    Coursology Extractor Tampermonkey Script

    This script runs on Coursology article pages and adds the "Copy Article MD" button at the bottom-right corner of your screen.

    Quick Setup Instructions:
    1. Install the Tampermonkey extension in Chrome/Firefox/Brave/Edge.
    2. Open Tampermonkey and click Create a new script.
    3. Click the button below to copy the code, replace everything in Tampermonkey with it, and press Ctrl+S to save.
    4. Open any article on Coursology — the blue "Copy Article MD" button will appear at the bottom-right!
